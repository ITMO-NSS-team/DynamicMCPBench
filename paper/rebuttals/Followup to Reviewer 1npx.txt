Follow-up to Reviewer 1npx

Both commitments to you are measured and reported, including the one that came back against us.

THE REFERENCE-VALIDATION GATE

You asked whether the distiller omits necessary effects. The gate is now implemented, enforced at generation time, and the class it governs is measured rather than asserted. Over 1,845 specs: **zero orphans**, 1,578 reference-consistent, 267 flagged (103 where the demanded value appears nowhere, 137 where it appears out of scope, 27 with no final message).

On the 750-task slice it governs **104 tasks, 13.9%**:

| | tasks | pass rate | never solved by any model |
|---|---|---|---|
| reference-consistent | 646 | 55.2% | 86 |
| flagged | 104 | 11.8% | 59 |

Flagged tasks are **4.3x more likely to be unsolvable by every model** and account for **40.7% of the never-solved wall**. Removing them **changes no model's rank**: every model gains about six points of pass^3 and the ordering is untouched.

THE CROSS-MODEL CHECK, AND A RETRACTION

We told you we expected the conservatism to move the level rather than the ordering. Three further models spanning 7 to 49 pass^3, 240 judgments, six raters. It does not hold.

| model | pass^3 | human passes a run the scorer failed |
|---|---|---|
| gemma4-31b | 42.5 | **66.7%** |
| qwen3.6-35b (baseline) | 48.5 | 49.9% |
| qwen3-8b | 22.1 | 25.0% |
| smollm3-3b | 7.2 | **8.3%** |

An eightfold spread, and reconstructed human pass rates swap the top pair: 81.6% for gemma4-31b against 74.2% for qwen3.6-35b, where the scorer has 44.9 and 54.0.

The effect decomposes. Capable models get further, so their failures land on `value_produced`, where the tool work was done but the final message missed the evidence predicate; humans pass 64% of those against 19% when a `tool_effect` is unmet. But standardising to a common failure mix leaves 45 of the 58 points, so composition is a quarter of it. The rest looks like unlisted equivalent paths: among `tool_effect`-unmet failures, smollm3-3b averages half a successful call on runs a human passed, so it is not judged harshly, it did nothing. gemma4-31b averages three and still misses the checkpoint. That is the equivalence-set recall limit we flagged to you as unmeasured, now measured.

The scorer is not the variable: Tier-1 is deterministic code, identical for every model, and distiller families are evenly spread across the samples.

WHAT THIS CHANGES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

Accuracies are still lower bounds, as the paper says, but the bound is tighter for weaker models, so the published spread is compressed and two models a few points apart cannot be ordered by it. We will say exactly that.

It does not touch what the paper argues: ground truth here is a set of observable effects rather than an answer string or a tool list, and that is what survives live, stateful data. This measures our instrument's resolution and gives both repairs an address, relax `value_produced` matching and widen equivalence sets while measuring their recall. Neither can lower a score; both raise the floor unevenly, which is the finding.

The gradient is not a small-sample artefact: the extreme pair separates at p = 2e-11 by Fisher's exact test with disjoint intervals. What 60 cards per model will not carry is the specific swap, which turns on 3.6 points, or the pass-stratum rates, which rest on 10 cards each.
