Three of the things we said would come in the camera-ready are done, and two of them turned out larger than the promise.

THE VALIDATOR, TIGHTENED

Promised: fix the one task with a missing effect and tighten the reference validator. Done, and the class it governs is now measured rather than asserted. Over 1,845 specs: zero orphans, 1,578 reference-consistent, 267 flagged.

On the 750-task slice the gate governs 104 tasks, 13.9%:

| | tasks | pass rate | never solved by any model |
|---|---|---|---|
| reference-consistent | 646 | 55.2% | 86 |
| flagged | 104 | 11.8% | 59 |

Flagged tasks are 4.3x more likely to be unsolvable by every model and account for 40.7% of the never-solved wall. Removing them changes no model's rank: every model gains about six points of pass^3 and the ordering is untouched. So the defect is real, bounded, and does not touch the comparison the paper rests on.

TIER-2 OVERRIDE RATES, PER CATEGORY

Promised: per-category rates in the camera-ready. Done, and across four judge families rather than one. 4,500 Tier-1 tool_effect failures, 300 per category from a population of 45,155, the same items to every family, prompt and schema held fixed.

| judge | override rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| claude-haiku-4.5 (our default) | 4.93% | 4.34 - 5.61 |
| gemma-4-26b | 5.42% | 4.80 - 6.12 |
| llama-3.3-70b | 5.33% | 4.71 - 6.03 |
| mistral-small-3.2-24b | 5.71% | 5.07 - 6.43 |

A 0.78 point spread with fully overlapping intervals. Three of the four have open weights and authored no part of the corpus, so the panel needs no paid key to reproduce. Per category the rate runs 1.83 percent on hard_neg to 9.83 percent on sibling, an order of magnitude more variation than the judge axis, and in the direction the taxonomy predicts.

Nine failures in ten are unanimous NO; a majority overrides 3.85 percent. Since Tier-2 only ever turns a failure into a pass, that is also the bound on how far any judge configuration could have moved the paper's numbers, and only upward.
