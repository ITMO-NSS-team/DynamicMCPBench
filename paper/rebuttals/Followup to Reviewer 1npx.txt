THE REFERENCE-VALIDATION GATE

The gate is implemented and enforced at generation time. Over 1,845 specs: zero orphans, 1,578 reference-consistent, 267 flagged.

On the 750-task slice it governs 104 tasks, 13.9%:

| | tasks | pass rate | never solved by any model |
|---|---|---|---|
| reference-consistent | 646 | 55.2% | 86 |
| flagged | 104 | 11.8% | 59 |

Flagged tasks are 4.3x more likely to be unsolvable by every model and account for 40.7% of the never-solved wall. Removing them changes no model's rank: every model gains about six points of pass^3 and the ordering is untouched.

TIER-2 OVERRIDE RATES, PER CATEGORY AND PER JUDGE FAMILY

4,500 Tier-1 tool_effect failures, 300 per category from a population of 45,155, the same items to every family, prompt and schema held fixed.

| judge | override rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| claude-haiku-4.5 (our default) | 4.93% | 4.34 - 5.61 |
| gemma-4-26b | 5.42% | 4.80 - 6.12 |
| llama-3.3-70b | 5.33% | 4.71 - 6.03 |
| mistral-small-3.2-24b | 5.71% | 5.07 - 6.43 |

A 0.78 point spread with fully overlapping intervals. Three of the four have open weights and authored no part of the corpus, so the panel reproduces without a paid key.

Per category the rate runs 1.83% on hard_neg to 9.83% on sibling, an order of magnitude more variation than the judge axis, and in the direction the taxonomy predicts: sibling seeds interchangeable tools on the same server, hard_neg seeds adversarially mined near-duplicates.

Nine failures in ten are unanimous NO; a majority overrides 3.85%. Since Tier-2 only ever turns a failure into a pass, that is the bound on how far any judge configuration could move the paper's numbers, and only upward.
