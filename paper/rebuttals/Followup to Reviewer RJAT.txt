DRIFT SEPARATED FROM TRANSIENT FAILURE

The attributing classifier is built and the refresh re-run over 113 servers and 12 domains, against 3 in the paper. A 38-fold widening.

32.6% of effects still reproduce identically, against 36.0% narrow. The composition of the remainder:

| outcome | share |
|---|---|
| identical | 32.6% |
| drifted, the call succeeds and the result changed | 67.0% |
| attributably broken | 0.4% |

Four calls, all schema drift, all on one server, zero state decay. 320 more failed unattributably and are excluded rather than counted as breakage. The 32% flagged as an upper bound resolves to 0.4%.

Drift is the whole phenomenon, which sharpens the argument for cached replay rather than merely supporting it: the servers are not falling over, they are quietly returning different answers. Per domain, dev_tools 89.7% against science_health 19.1%, so live re-scoring would be unfair and unfairly weighted by subject matter.

The preflight derives credential, file, writable and table preconditions from the reference trace and quarantines a task whose environment cannot be provisioned before any live call, so it never reaches the agent-failure column.

Four models, k in {4, 8, 16, 32}, a hierarchical router and the flat catalog, over 150 depth-balanced tasks with committed ids.

Budget helps and never closes the gap: 12 to 19 points gained from k=4 to k=32, still 7 to 14 below curated. The binding constraint is reachability, not ranking. The retriever surfaces every required tool for 36.7, 48.0, 62.7 and 75.3 percent of tasks at k = 4, 8, 16, 32, and of the 1,064 unreachable cells zero passed. Three attempts at k=8 recover 16 to 22 percent of the deficit. Across the four models the curated-to-open gap is 12.8 points on short chains and 19.5 on long.

JUDGE FAMILIES, PER CATEGORY

4,500 Tier-1 tool_effect failures, 300 per category from a population of 45,155, the same items to every family, prompt and schema held fixed.

| judge | override rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| claude-haiku-4.5 (our default) | 4.93% | 4.34 - 5.61 |
| gemma-4-26b | 5.42% | 4.80 - 6.12 |
| llama-3.3-70b | 5.33% | 4.71 - 6.03 |
| mistral-small-3.2-24b | 5.71% | 5.07 - 6.43 |

A 0.78 point spread, fully overlapping intervals, pairwise agreement 94.5 to 97.0 percent. Three of the four have open weights and authored no part of the corpus, so the panel reproduces without a paid key.

Per category the rate runs 1.83% on hard_neg to 9.83% on sibling, an order of magnitude more variation than the family axis: sibling seeds interchangeable tools on the same server, hard_neg seeds adversarially mined near-duplicates.

Nine failures in ten are unanimous NO; a majority overrides 3.85%. Since Tier-2 only turns a failure into a pass, that bounds how far any judge configuration could move our numbers, and only upward.
