\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We propose a benchmark generation and evaluation shape that \emph{does
not grade the answer} and \emph{does not require a tool-list ground
truth}. The trace is the primitive; effect checkpoints are the
contract. Empirically, the alternative shapes both produce rankings
that disagree sharply with the trace-grounded one (RQ1,
\S\ref{sec:rq1}; RQ2, \S\ref{sec:rq2}). Trace-property regression
isolates \emph{task dynamism} as the dominant failure driver (RQ3,
\S\ref{sec:rq3}). The remaining piece~--- scorer-vs-human
calibration~--- is gated behind a pre-registered annotation pass (RQ4,
\S\ref{sec:rq4}). Code, manifests, and released datasets ship as the
\texttt{dmcp} package (current repository) and a HuggingFace dataset
(pending the leaderboard run).
