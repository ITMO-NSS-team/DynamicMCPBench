\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{Motivation}

LLM agents over Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers are an increasingly
real deployment surface; their benchmarks are not. The two prevailing
shapes for ``how do we know an agent did the right thing?'' both lean on
proxies that wobble under live data:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Final-answer string matching} (the common-case ``score
    the reply against the reference answer''). Works for static
    fact-recall, breaks the moment the answer depends on the current
    world: today's weather, this morning's commit, the live database row.
  \item \textbf{Tool-list recall} (AgentGraphBench and its
    descendants). The ground truth is the set of tools the agent
    \emph{should have called}. Diagnosed by AGB itself as $\sim$50\,\%
    noise~--- \emph{unnecessary tools} drift in because tool lists are
    not observable from prompts alone.
\end{itemize}

We propose to \emph{change the organizing primitive} of the benchmark
from \emph{answer} and \emph{tool set} to the \textbf{execution trace}.
The trace is the only object that is unambiguously observable (we
recorded it) and unambiguously faithful (every tool in a trace was
actually invoked).

\subsection{Claims and contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{A forward generator} (\texttt{dmcp explore} $\to$
    \texttt{dmcp distill}) that turns a live successful trajectory into
    a path-agnostic \texttt{TaskSpec} made of effect \emph{checkpoints}
    and \emph{minefields}, with \emph{equivalence sets} over tools that
    genuinely substitute (Method, \S\ref{sec:method}). The headline
    structural property absent in prior-art generators: forward
    distillation emits mean $|equivalence\_set| > 1.0$, while
    graph-sampling and direct generate-then-verify are singletons by
    construction (Experiment~e4.3, \S\ref{sec:rq2}).
  \item \textbf{Trace/effect alignment} as the scorer
    (\texttt{evaluator.py}, Tier-1 deterministic) with an optional LLM
    judge (Tier-2). Never grades the final answer; never requires
    step-for-step trajectory match (\S\ref{sec:method:eval}). On
    56\,$\times$\,3 evals: switching from trace-align to answer-match
    inverts the model ranking, Kendall's $\tau = \mathbf{-0.816}$;
    answer-match's false-pass rate is $\mathbf{63\%}$ versus
    trace-align's $\mathbf{1.8\%}$ false-fail rate
    (Experiment~e4.4, \S\ref{sec:rq1}).
  \item \textbf{A trace-property failure model} identifying
    \texttt{dynamism\_live} as the dominant driver of failure across
    candidate models (pooled $\beta = -3.110$, drop-loglik~$+6.606$,
    $\approx 70\%$ of pooled importance on the v3 substrate;
    Experiment~e4.5, \S\ref{sec:rq3}).
  \item \textbf{A living-bench protocol} for measuring decay between
    distillation and re-evaluation (\texttt{dmcp refresh},
    \S\ref{sec:method:refresh}; deployment-time numbers pending an
    extended run).
  \item \textbf{A reproducible substrate} of vetted, sandboxed, live
    MCP servers (currently 16; target $\geq 100$,
    \S\ref{sec:substrate}; scale gated on the ongoing crawl).
\end{enumerate}
