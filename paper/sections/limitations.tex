\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This section is mandatory for the EMNLP 2026 Industry Track and does
not count toward the 6-page main-text limit.

\paragraph{Substrate scale.} The numbers in
\S\ref{sec:rq1}--\S\ref{sec:rq3} are on the 16-server v3 substrate.
The paper-target scale ($\geq 100$ servers) is in progress; we re-derive
every \S\ref{sec:experiments} number on the larger substrate before
final submission. Until then RQ3's collinearity
(\texttt{state\_coupling} $\equiv$ \texttt{dynamism\_stateful} in v3)
limits per-feature decomposition.

\paragraph{Human consensus is pending.} RQ4 ships the harness and the
pre-registered protocol, not the $\kappa$ / $\alpha$ numbers. Until
annotators complete the pass, \S\ref{sec:rq4} carries the placeholder
verdict.

\paragraph{LLM-judge non-determinism.} Tier-2 is the only
non-deterministic scoring path; we mitigate by pinning model and
temperature and reporting Tier-1 numbers alongside Tier-2. The
Tier-1 / Tier-2 split is reproducible via the same eval JSONL.

\paragraph{Distiller bias.} The distiller is an LLM; we pin it to a
tool-call schema at temperature $0$, but it can still under- or
over-emit checkpoints in edge cases. The mitigation is the
trace-vs-spec reviewable record (\texttt{TaskSpec.notes}) plus the
manual review that graduated v3 to a released dataset.

\paragraph{Substrate coverage.} The local manifest leans heavily on
English-language public-API servers; cross-lingual and proprietary
sources are not represented. Generalising the dynamism stratification
to those settings is straightforward in principle but unmeasured here.

\paragraph{Operational risk of live exploration.} The forward generator
calls live MCP servers during distillation. We mandate
\texttt{sandbox=true} for \texttt{stateful\_write} servers, but this
guards against in-substrate corruption, not against rate-limits or
upstream service-of-record terms. Industry adopters must validate
exploration policies against their compliance envelope before running
the generator at scale.
