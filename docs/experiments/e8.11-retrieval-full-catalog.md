# E8.11 — retrieval over the full catalog vs the curated eval pool

## Question / hypothesis

Reviewers of the EMNLP submission (RJAT, sJ7917) object that the headline
condition hands the candidate the tools it needs plus eight distractors, so it
measures planning and execution but not **tool discovery**. Their claim is that
a practitioner faces a large tool universe, either flat or behind a retriever,
and that our conclusions may not transfer to that setting.

**Question.** How much of the accuracy level, and how much of the chain-length
structure, survives when the candidate must retrieve its tools from the entire
catalog instead of being handed a curated pool?

## Method

Condition under test: `--pool full --architecture rag --rag-k 8`. The eval pool
is required tools plus every other tool in the catalog (**1,168 tools** built
from the released reference traces via `ToolCatalog.from_traces`); the RAG front
end then embeds the task prompt and every tool's `"name: description"` and
exposes only the **top 8** by cosine similarity. So the candidate still sees
eight tools, but nothing guarantees the right ones are among them.

Baseline: the **released** per-run verdicts for the same model on the same
`task_id`s (`leaderboard_e8.10d/verdicts/evals_qwen3.7-max.jsonl`, pool `target`,
`p_alt` 0.5, pool size 8). No baseline compute was spent, and the comparison is
matched task-for-task. Both sides are single-attempt.

- Model: `qwen/qwen3.7-max` (rank 1 on the headline leaderboard).
- Slice: 150 tasks drawn from the 750-task evaluation slice, **balanced 50/50/50**
  across chain depth (short 1-2, medium 3-4, long 5+), `random.seed(0)`.
- `--budget 12 --repeat 1 --replay`. Six shards in parallel, round-robin
  interleaved by depth bucket so any prefix stays balanced.

### Reproduce

```
uv run dmcp eval <subset>.jsonl --manifest manifests/servers.json \
  --replay --reference-traces <corpus>/traces.jsonl \
  --model qwen/qwen3.7-max --pool full --architecture rag --rag-k 8 \
  --budget 12 --repeat 1 --output evals/rag.jsonl
uv run python scripts/rag_compare.py --model qwen3.7-max
```

## Pre-registered decision rule

Positive for the framework's external validity if the **ordering by chain length
is preserved** under retrieval, i.e. accuracy still falls monotonically with
required chain depth. Negative if retrieval flattens or inverts that ordering,
which would mean the headline condition manufactured the paper's central result.
The absolute level was **not** pre-registered — a level drop is expected and is
not itself a negative outcome.

## Results

Matched on 150 tasks, single attempt:

| scope | n | retrieval, full catalog | 95% CI | curated pool | delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| all | 150 | **36.7%** | [29.4, 44.6] | 57.3% | −20.7 pp |
| short (1-2) | 50 | 64.0% | [50.1, 75.9] | 62.0% | +2.0 pp |
| medium (3-4) | 50 | 30.0% | [19.1, 43.8] | 74.0% | −44.0 pp |
| long (5+) | 50 | 16.0% | [8.3, 28.5] | 36.0% | −20.0 pp |

The retrieval curve is cleanly monotone in chain depth: **64.0 → 30.0 → 16.0**.

Failure composition, over the 274 unmet checkpoints: **175 (64%) are tools the
agent never called at all**, 90 (33%) are demanded evidence absent from the
answer, 9 (3%) other. Under retrieval the dominant new failure mode is therefore
discovery, not reasoning. Replay cache misses averaged 4.2 per task, with at
least one on 96 of 150 tasks.

**Sampling-bias control.** Completion order correlates with task difficulty
(short tasks finish first), so partial results are biased. At 30 completed the
baseline restricted to completed tasks read 66.7% against 57.3% on the full
slice, a +9.3 pp skew; by 78 completed the gap had closed to 0.9 pp. All numbers
above are on the complete 150 and every comparison is matched task-for-task, not
against the full-slice baseline.

## Result: **positive**

The decision rule is met. Retrieval over a 1,168-tool catalog costs about 21
points of absolute accuracy, but the cost is **entirely compositional**:
single-step tasks are unaffected (+2.0 pp, well inside the interval), while
multi-step tasks lose 44 and 20 points. Open-universe exposure does not
introduce a new difficulty axis and does not manufacture the paper's
chain-length finding — it sharpens it.

## Conclusion

The headline conclusion transfers to the setting practitioners actually face,
and the reviewers' concern is quantified rather than waved away: the level is
materially lower under retrieval, the ordering is unchanged, and the extra
failures are dominated by tools the agent never found.

Scope: one model, one retriever (embedding top-8), one slice, single attempt.
Not run here: the flat full-catalog condition (`--architecture flat`), the
hierarchical router (`hier`), other `rag-k`, other models, pass^3. Those are the
natural camera-ready extensions.
