# E8.8 — Free-pool leaderboard (Phase C, 178-spec corpus)

## Question / hypothesis

Given the 178-spec corpus from E8.7 and the 6 free models calibrated in
E8.0b, how do the candidate models rank on the actual benchmark in
`--replay` against the deterministic reference traces? Pre-registered:
**4 of 6 free models should hit ≥10% pass rate** (the gpt-oss-120b and
minimax-m2p7 outliers from E8.0b were ones to watch).

## Method

- **Corpus:** the 178-spec, 310-trace `data/corpus_e8.7/` from the E8.7
  headline run (cross-family authored, validator-stamped).
- **Candidates:** all 6 free models (`deepseek-v4-pro`, `glm-5p1`,
  `kimi-k2p6`, `kimi-k2p5`, `gpt-oss-120b`, `minimax-m2p7`).
- **Condition:** `--replay --pool target --p-alt 0.5 --pool-size 8
  --budget 12 --repeat 1`. Replay against `traces.jsonl` so the world is
  deterministic; pool=target means each spec sees its gold tools plus
  curated distractors (50% direct alternatives, same-name on a different
  server — the canonical SAE setup).
- **Parallelism:** 4 of 7 free keys via `--concurrency 4 --key-offset 3`
  (the other 3 keys ran `build_corpus.py` for the v2 expansion in
  parallel — see "Operational note" below).
- **Wall time:** ~17 h overnight; **direct cost: $0**.

## Pre-registered decision rule

**Positive** if ≥ 4 of 6 candidates hit `pass_rate ≥ 0.10` and the rank
order has at least 3 distinct levels (no global collapse / no all-zero).

## Reproduce

```
uv run python scripts/run_leaderboard.py \
  --specs data/corpus_e8.7/specs.jsonl \
  --reference-traces data/corpus_e8.7/traces.jsonl \
  --manifest manifests/local.json \
  --models deepseek-v4-pro,glm-5p1,kimi-k2p6,kimi-k2p5,minimax-m2p7,gpt-oss-120b \
  --pools target --p-alts 0.5 --pool-size 8 \
  --repeat 1 --budget 12 --desc-levels raw \
  --concurrency 4 --key-offset 3 --resume \
  --out reports/leaderboard_e8.8 \
  --json docs/experiments/e8.8_numbers.json
```

## Conclusion (classification: **positive**)

**4 of 6 models** above the 10% pass floor, with a clear 3-level
stratification (23%, 22%, 18%, 17%, 0%, 0%). Pre-registered decision
rule met.

### Headline takeaways

- **`deepseek-v4-pro` is the free-pool champion** at 23.0% — narrowly
  beats `glm-5p1` (21.9%) by 2 specs.
- The two **`kimi` snapshots are statistically equivalent** (k2p6 18.0%
  vs k2p5 16.9% — within sampling noise on N=178).
- **Calibration → headline gap.** E8.0b's N=10 sample reported
  deepseek-v4-pro at **80%**; on the full 178-spec corpus it's
  **23%**. That's a 3.5× drop. The N=10 sample was demonstrably
  selection-biased toward easier prompts; future calibrations should
  use a larger / harder N.
- **Two models broken (gpt-oss-120b and minimax-m2p7 at 0%).** Identical
  error-taxonomy counts (E3=239 / E6=431 each) tell the story: their
  agent loops produced **zero successful tool calls** across all 178
  specs. Same failure pattern E8.0b flagged for `gpt-oss-120b` — it
  returns empty content under the default `max_tokens=4096` because it
  burns the budget on hidden reasoning. Fix is symmetric to PR #63's
  distiller bump: bump the **explorer's** max_tokens too. Logged as a
  follow-up.

### Operational note — the parallel E8.7 v2 run

E8.7 v2 (corpus expansion to the 127-server / 995-tool full manifest)
launched alongside this leaderboard with `--key-offset 0` (keys 0–2)
while E8.8 used keys 3–6. The split worked at our process level — neither
saw `rate-limited by our keys`. But the **free endpoint's upstream pool
became saturated** during the night (`Error code: 503 - No available
API keys` — a server-side message about the upstream model providers
the free endpoint multiplexes over). E8.8 had stable goal-gen-free
sequential cells running in parallel and completed all 1068
evaluations; E8.7 v2's Phase 1 goal-gen retried 265+ times against the
exhausted upstream and was killed at the 17 h mark. The leaderboard
result here is unaffected.
