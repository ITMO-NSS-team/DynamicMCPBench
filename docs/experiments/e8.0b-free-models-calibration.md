# E8.0b — Free-models provider + recalibration

## Question / hypothesis

User 2026-06-04 obtained access to a private free-tier endpoint with 6
agentic-tool-use models. **Question:** does the free pool match the
paid-pool accuracy seen in E8.0a closely enough that the bulk of E8.7 / E8.8
can run at \$0, with paid OpenRouter models reserved only for capabilities
the free pool can't cover?

## Method

- **Free pool (user-locked):** `deepseek-v4-pro`, `kimi-k2p6`, `kimi-k2p5`,
  `glm-5p1`, `gpt-oss-120b`, `minimax-m2p7`.
- **Same N=10 sample** as E8.0a (first 10 specs of `specs/v3.jsonl`,
  mixed-domain: time, fetch, git, sqlite).
- **Same condition** as E8.0a: `dmcp eval --replay --pool target
  --pool-size 8 --p-alt 0.5 --budget 12` — so the per-model accuracies
  here are directly comparable to the paid table.
- **Provider routing:** `dmcp/providers.py::resolve()` exact-matches
  bare-name ids to the FREE provider; everything else stays on OpenRouter.
  `OpenRouterClient.__init__` consults the registry when caller doesn't
  pin `base_url`/`api_key`, so the 30+ existing callsites Just Work.
- **Concurrency:** the user provided 3 API keys
  (`FREE_MODELS_API_KEY[, _2, _3]`); `cost_calibration.py --concurrency 3`
  round-robins them across cells via per-subprocess `env_override`. 6
  models × 3 lanes = 2 parallel waves, ~½ the serial wall time.
- **Pricing:** free models pinned at `ModelPrice(0.0, 0.0)` in
  `dmcp/pricing.py` so `unknown_price` doesn't trip and extrapolation reads
  honest \$0 without special-casing.

## Pre-registered decision rule

The recommended *combined* pool is: every free model that ≥ the median
paid-pool accuracy (60% on E8.0a) is kept; paid OpenRouter models are
added only when the free pool can't supply a needed capability tier
(frontier reference, distinct family for cross-family generation).

## Reproduce

```
uv run python scripts/cost_calibration.py \
  --free-pool --concurrency 3 --n 10 --no-live-prices \
  --specs specs/v3.jsonl --reference-traces traces/v3.jsonl \
  --manifest manifests/servers.json \
  --out reports/cost_calibration_free \
  --json docs/experiments/e8.0b_numbers.json
```

### Extrapolated total \$ (full pool)

All cells $0 across the {600, 1100} × pass^k ∈ {1, 3, 5} grid — the free
endpoint covers the entire calibration. **The pool total for E8.7 (1100
× pass^3) on the free pool is \$0.00**, vs the paid \$308.75 from E8.0a.

### Latency note (worth flagging)

p95 ranges 113-162 seconds — significantly worse than the paid pool's
~5-24 sec range. The free endpoint likely has cold-start or queueing
behavior; budget for long wall-clock on the headline runs. p50 is more
representative (5-62 sec).

## Conclusion (classification: **positive**)

**The free pool replaces 80%+ of the planned paid spend.** Three free
models hit or exceed the paid pool's median accuracy (deepseek-v4-pro 80%,
glm-5p1 80%, kimi-k2p6 70%); two more (kimi-k2p5, minimax-m2p7) at 60%
are still useful for diversity; gpt-oss-120b at 40% is the only weak link.

### Recommended combined pool for E8.7 / E8.8

**Free tier (4 keepers, ~zero cost):**

| # | Model | Why kept | Family |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `deepseek-v4-pro` | Top accuracy at \$0; matches sonnet-4.6 | deepseek |

**Paid tier (2-3 add-ons for capability coverage, ~\$45 for E8.7):**

| # | Model | Why kept | E8.7 \$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | `qwen/qwen3-coder-plus` | Adds Qwen family (free pool has none); tool specialist | $19.31 |

**Total E8.7 (1100 × pass^3) on the combined 6-model pool (4 free + 2 paid): ~\$55** —
vs \$308.75 on the pure-paid E8.0a recommendation. **5.6× cost reduction.**
With the optional sonnet anchor, ~\$198.

### Drops & findings (non-blocking)

- **`kimi-k2p5` and `gpt-oss-120b`** dropped from the headline pool —
  kimi-k2p5 is superseded by k2p6 (same family, higher acc); gpt-oss-120b
  at 40% is a separate-family probe (`openai-oss`) that may still be useful
  for a diversity ablation, but not headline.
- **Free-endpoint latency** is high (p95 110-162 sec). Worth running the
  headline E8.7 with `--concurrency 3` (or higher if more keys land) to
  keep wall time tractable. A 1100-spec corpus per model at 30 sec
  median = ~9 hours sequential, ~3 hours over 3 lanes.
- **Pareto picker still has the broken-frontier-cluster artifact** at \$0
  costs — when every model ties at \$0 cost, only the first-listed gets
  on the frontier because the "step up in accuracy" rule trivially holds
  on the first row. Same fix as the E8.0a flag (`runs > 0 and
  prompt_tokens > 0`) plus a tie-break on accuracy. Logged; manual override
  applied above.
