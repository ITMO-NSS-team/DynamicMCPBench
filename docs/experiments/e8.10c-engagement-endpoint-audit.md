# E8.10c — endpoint vs behaviour audit of low-scoring models (E8.8b)

## Question / hypothesis

The E8.8b leaderboard's bottom ranks (kimi-k2.6, claude-haiku-4.5,
deepseek-v4-pro) scored 14–21% pass^3. **Are these genuine capability
gaps, or endpoint/harness artifacts?** Triggered by the observation that
low scorers fail mostly with **zero agent tool-calls**.

## Method

Post-hoc on E8.8b + cheap targeted re-probes:

1. **Log scan** for API-error signatures across lanes.
2. **Structured decomposition**: per model, fraction of *failed* runs
   with zero `agent_call_count` / zero `agent_call_success_count`
   (from `EvaluationResult.summary`).
3. **Engagement-vs-score** correlation across the 8 models.
4. **max_tokens probe**: re-call kimi on its zero-call tasks at
   `max_tokens` 4096 vs 16384 — does truncation explain it?
5. **Faithful re-probe**: rebuild the **exact eval pool surface**
   (`build_eval_pool(..., seed=task_id.int % 2**31)` +
   `pool_to_tool_surface`) for 20 deepseek zero-call tasks and re-call —
   does deepseek engage under the identical condition?

## Results

### No endpoint errors in the logs

The grep "errors" (~294/lane) were `429`/`5xx` **substrings in task
data** (identical counts across all models — replay feeds the same
world). Searching for real signatures (`APIError`, `RuntimeError`,
`rate limit`, traceback) returns **zero**. The in-client retries
(#88/#91/#95) caught nothing because there was nothing to catch.

### Failures are zero-tool-call, concentrated in the bottom ranks

| model | per-run pass% | tool-engaged% | zero-call% |
|---|---|---|---|
| glm-5.1 | 55 | 94 | 6 |
| minimax-m3 | 40 | 72 | 27 |
| grok-4.3 | 38 | 95 | 1 |
| qwen3.7-max | 35 | 61 | 38 |
| gpt-5.4-mini | 29 | 70 | 6 |
| deepseek-v4-pro | 27 | 48 | 51 |
| claude-haiku-4.5 | 23 | 46 | 53 |
| kimi-k2.6 | 17 | 35 | 64 |

**Pearson r(tool-engagement, pass) = 0.875.** The score floor is an
engagement floor.

### Not truncation

kimi zero-call tasks: `finish='stop'` (not `'length'`) at 250–650
completion tokens; raising `max_tokens` 4096→16384 leaves tool_calls at
0. The reasoning-token-exhaustion hypothesis is **refuted**.

### It is a transient provider-routing artifact (the key result)

Re-running **deepseek-v4-pro** on 20 tasks it scored **zero tool-calls**
on in the leaderboard, under the **exact eval pool surface + seed**:

> **engaged a tool on 17/20 (85%)**, `finish='tool_calls'`.

The original lane recorded zero calls on all 20. So ~85% of deepseek's
zero-calls are **transient** — under the 8-lane concurrent load,
OpenRouter routed deepseek-v4-pro requests to a provider/path that
returned content **without** `tool_calls` (HTTP 200, no error), and the
agent stopped. A non-faithful probe (reference surface) gave 90%,
confirming the pool surface is not the cause.

### Genuine behaviour also exists (a minority)

Some zero-calls are real: kimi on an under-specified prompt ("rewrite a
customer-support reply" with no reply text) **consistently asks the user
for clarification** instead of calling the enhance-prompt tool — a true
agentic-engagement gap, and arguably a *reasonable* response to a weak
spec (a corpus-quality nuance for RQ4).

## Conclusion (classification: **negative — known artifact, fix required**)

The bottom of the E8.8b leaderboard is **contaminated by a transient
provider-routing artifact**, not endpoint errors and not (mostly) real
capability. Concretely:

- **Top ranks are reliable** — glm-5.1 (94% engaged) and grok-4.3 (95%)
  are unaffected; the headline winner stands.
- **Ranks 4–8 are understated** — deepseek-v4-pro in particular is badly
  penalised (≈85% of its zero-calls are recoverable on re-run). Its true
  position is likely much higher.
- The artifact is **silent**: HTTP 200 + `finish='stop'` + no
  `tool_calls`, so neither the retry layer nor the logs flag it.

### Recommended fix (for the re-run that produces the quotable board)

1. **Pin OpenRouter providers** to those that honour tool schemas:
   `extra_body={"provider": {"require_parameters": true}}` (and/or an
   explicit allow-list / `allow_fallbacks: false`) in `llm.py`.
2. **Capture `finish_reason` + candidate traces** (`--candidate-traces-out`)
   so zero-call runs are auditable, not invisible.
3. Optionally **treat a turn-1 "no tool_calls with tools offered and a
   tool-effect task" as retryable** in the agent loop.
4. **Re-run the affected models** (≥ ranks 4–8); the top two can be
   reused. Lower concurrency or more keys reduces load-routing pressure.

Until then, E8.8b carries a validity caveat (added to that report).
